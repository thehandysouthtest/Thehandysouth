WHY THE HANDY SOUTH

Sometimes you just need somebody who can help.

Finding the right person for a small job can be harder than the job itself. We’re building a simple local service that makes asking for help easier.

Have a skill? We’ll eventually make it easy for people who want to help others to find paid opportunities, too.
